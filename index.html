Materials

Clay, fire, glaze

Clay bodyStoneware, earthenware
FiringCone 4 – Cone 6
Glaze finishSatin, matte, textured
PaletteBeige, grey, sage, rust
StudioSeattle, WA

Most work is thrown in stoneware, chosen for its warmth and everyday durability, and fired between cone 4 and cone 6 depending on the clay body and glaze. Earthenware appears in a few softer, lower-fire pieces where the material calls for it.

Glazes stay close to a quiet, neutral register — warm beige, soft grey, muted sage, and a deep rust I keep coming back to. I favor finishes that feel natural under the fingers: satin and matte over anything glossy.

Each glaze is layered and tested in small batches before it goes on a full piece. I leave room for the kiln to do part of the work — small breaks and shifts in the surface are kept, not corrected.
