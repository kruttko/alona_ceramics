Collaborate

Open to

I like working with cafés, restaurants, and small hospitality spaces on custom tableware — mugs, plates, and serving pieces built around a specific menu or space. I'm also open to pairing up with other makers on limited joint runs, and to editorial or styling work where the pieces need to hold up on camera as well as in the hand.

If you're planning a workshop, pop-up, or market and think the work would be a fit, I'd love to hear the details — space, dates, and what you have in mind.
